FAQ

Is there a Thanksgiving emoji?

There isn't one official "Thanksgiving" emoji, but 🦃 (turkey) is the one most people use. It's often paired with 🥧 (pie), 🍂 (fallen leaf), 🍁 (maple leaf), 🌽 (corn), and 🙏 (folded hands) for a Thanksgiving feel.

How do I copy and paste Thanksgiving emoji combos?

Press and hold a combo on your phone (or highlight it on a computer), choose Copy, then paste it into your message, caption, or bio. They're standard emoji, so they work on most devices and apps.

Do these Thanksgiving emoji combos work on iPhone?

Yes. They're regular Unicode emoji, so they work on iPhone, Android, Windows, and Mac. They may look slightly different depending on the device.

Can I make a Thanksgiving Emoji Kitchen mashup?

Turkey, pie, pumpkin, corn, and folded hands have no Emoji Kitchen mashups. Fall leaves like 🍂 and 🍁, and some food emoji such as 🍗, do. Open the mixer, pick a listed pair, and save the image if a preview appears.

Can I put an Emoji Kitchen mashup in my bio?

No. Mashups are images, and bios only accept text. Use the copy-and-paste Thanksgiving combos above in your bio instead.

Is Emoji Kitchen Hub the same as Google's Emoji Kitchen?

No. Emoji Kitchen Hub is an unofficial, fan-made website. It is not run by Google and is not affiliated with Google, Gboard, or Apple. Google's Emoji Kitchen is a separate feature in Gboard, and the available pairs can differ.
