Emoji Kitchen mashups, like a crying-laughing cat or a heart-eyed pumpkin, make great Instagram Story decorations and DM reactions. Instagram doesn't have Emoji Kitchen built in, but getting a mashup into your Story, chat, or post only takes a minute once you know the trick: save the mashup as an image, then add it like any other photo or sticker.

This guide covers Instagram Stories, direct messages (DMs), feed posts, and your bio, on both iPhone and Android, plus fixes for the most common problems.

Quick note: Emoji Kitchen Hub (emojikitchenhub.com) is an unofficial, fan-made website. It is not run by Google and isn't affiliated with Google, Gboard, Instagram, or Meta. Google's own Emoji Kitchen feature in Gboard is a separate product, and the pairs it offers can differ from what you find here.

First: Emoji Kitchen mashups are images, not emoji

This is the key to everything below. A mashup isn't a new emoji character that you can type into a caption. It's a picture. That means:

  • You can add it anywhere Instagram accepts an image: Stories, DMs, and posts.
  • You can't type it inside a caption, comment, or bio. For those, use regular emoji or copy-and-paste emoji combos instead.
  • Your followers don't need any special app to see it. They just see the image.

Step 1: Make and save a mashup

You can do this on any phone or computer.

  1. Open the Emoji Kitchen Hub mixer.
  2. Pick two emoji. If the pair has a mashup, you'll see a preview.
  3. Save or download the image to your phone. On most phones, you can also long-press the image and choose to save it to Photos or your gallery.

Tip: Create an album called something like "Mashups" so your favorites are easy to find later.

On Android with Gboard, you can also find mashups through the keyboard's sticker suggestions. See our Emoji Kitchen on Gboard guide for how that works.

How to add an Emoji Kitchen mashup to an Instagram Story

Stories are the most popular place for mashups because you can resize, rotate, and place them anywhere.

  1. Open Instagram and start a new Story. Take a photo or pick a background from your camera roll.
  2. Tap the sticker icon at the top of the screen.
  3. Look for the photo sticker (it usually shows a small picture of your camera roll). Tap it.
  4. Choose the mashup you saved.
  5. Pinch to resize it, drag it into place, and rotate it with two fingers.
  6. Share your Story as usual.

Mashups saved as PNG files usually keep a transparent background, so they sit on your Story like a real sticker instead of a white box. If yours shows a background, see the troubleshooting section below.

Copy and paste on iPhone (alternative)

On iPhone, you can often copy an image and paste it straight into a Story:

  1. Open your saved mashup in Photos (or on Emoji Kitchen Hub), long-press it, and tap Copy.
  2. Go back to your Story, tap the screen to start typing, and paste.

If pasting doesn't work on your version of Instagram, use the photo sticker method above. For more iPhone-specific help, see our Emoji Kitchen on iPhone guide.

How to send an Emoji Kitchen mashup in Instagram DMs

Option 1: Send the saved image

  1. Open the chat in Instagram DMs.
  2. Tap the gallery or photo icon next to the message box.
  3. Pick your saved mashup and send it.

It will arrive as a picture. That's normal, since mashups are images.

Option 2: Use Gboard stickers on Android

If you use Gboard on Android, type an emoji in the DM message box and check whether Gboard shows mashup sticker suggestions above the keyboard. If it does, tap one to send it. Whether this works can depend on your Gboard and Instagram versions. If you don't see suggestions, use Option 1.

How to use a mashup in an Instagram post

  1. Save one or more mashups to your phone.
  2. Use them as part of your image: for example, add them to a photo in an editing app, or design a simple graphic with a mashup on a colored background.
  3. Upload it to Instagram like any post.

Keep it fun and simple: one big mashup on a clean background tends to read well in a small feed thumbnail.

Can I put Emoji Kitchen in my Instagram bio or captions?

Not as a mashup image. Bios, captions, comments, and usernames only accept text, so image stickers can't go there. You can still get a similar look with text emoji combinations:

Troubleshooting: Emoji Kitchen on Instagram

I can't find the photo sticker in Stories

Instagram moves things around between updates. Update the app, then look through the full sticker tray, scrolling if needed. If you still can't find it, try the copy-and-paste method on iPhone, or place the mashup on your background with a photo editing app first and then upload that image.

My mashup has a white or black box around it

Some save methods convert the image to JPG, which drops transparency. Try saving the mashup again as a PNG, or long-press and save the original image instead of taking a screenshot. Screenshots always include the background.

The mashup looks blurry

Very small images can look soft when stretched across a Story. Avoid making the sticker much bigger than it needs to be, and save the original image rather than a screenshot.

Gboard doesn't show mashup suggestions in DMs

Make sure Gboard is your active keyboard, update Gboard and Instagram, and try a different emoji, since not every emoji has mashups. Our Emoji Kitchen not working guide has more fixes. If nothing helps, send the saved image instead.

The two emoji I want don't have a mashup

Each mashup is designed artwork, so not every pair exists. Try a similar emoji or a different combination in the mixer.

FAQ

Does Instagram have Emoji Kitchen built in?

No. Instagram has its own stickers, GIFs, and effects, but Emoji Kitchen mashups aren't part of them. You can save a mashup as an image and add it to a Story with the photo sticker, or send it in a DM.

How do I add an Emoji Kitchen sticker to my Instagram Story?

Save the mashup to your phone, start a Story, tap the sticker icon, choose the photo sticker, and pick your mashup. Then resize and place it wherever you like.

Can I use Emoji Kitchen on Instagram on iPhone?

Yes. Gboard's built-in mashups are mainly an Android feature, but on iPhone you can make a mashup on Emoji Kitchen Hub, save it to Photos, and add it to a Story or DM like any image.

Why can't I put an Emoji Kitchen mashup in my Instagram bio?

Bios only accept text, and mashups are images. Use copy-and-paste emoji combos to decorate your bio instead.

Will my followers be able to see my Emoji Kitchen mashup?

Yes. A mashup is shared as an image, so anyone who can see your Story, post, or message can see it. They don't need Gboard or any special app.

Is Emoji Kitchen Hub the same as Google's Emoji Kitchen?
