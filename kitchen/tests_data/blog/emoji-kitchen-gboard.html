Emoji Kitchen Gboard: How to Use, Enable & Fix It

Want to mix two emojis on your Android keyboard but cannot find the right option? Emoji Kitchen makes those mashups possible, but the controls can look different after Gboard updates. This guide explains where the feature appears, how to create stickers, and what to check when suggestions are missing.

What Is Emoji Kitchen in Gboard?

Emoji Kitchen is Google’s sticker-mixing feature built into Gboard on Android. It combines supported emojis into designed mashups that can show a more specific mood, joke, object, or reaction than one standard emoji.

The result is not a new Unicode character. It is a sticker-style image, so it may appear larger than a normal emoji and can be shared like visual content.

Google first introduced Emoji Kitchen through Gboard and has kept adding new combinations. Google said in 2024 that Emoji Kitchen had more than 100,000 drawings. Later Android updates added new sticker combinations and easier ways to browse the library and save favorites.

How to Enable Emoji Kitchen on Gboard

The main requirement is to have an up-to-date Gboard set as your active Android keyboard. Google’s Gboard Help says you can install Gboard, open a typing app, and access keyboard settings from the features menu.

Use this setup checklist:

  1. Install or update Gboard from Google Play.
  2. Set Gboard as your active or default keyboard.
  3. Open an app with a text field, such as a messaging app.
  4. Open Gboard and tap its emoji button.
  5. If needed, open Gboard Settings and check the emoji, sticker, or suggestion options available on your version.

The exact menu wording can change. Do not worry if your screen does not match an older tutorial exactly. Gboard changes over time, so older screenshots may show a different layout.

How to Use Emoji Kitchen Gboard on Android

Using the feature is usually a short process once Gboard is active. Start inside an app that accepts sticker or image suggestions from the keyboard.

  1. Open a chat or text field. Tap the message box so Gboard appears.
  2. Open the emoji panel. Tap the emoji face on the keyboard.
  3. Choose your first emoji. A face, heart, animal, food item, or object is a good starting point.
  4. Pick a second compatible emoji. Gboard may show mashups above the emoji panel or in a suggestion area.
  5. Check the result. Not every pair produces a sticker, so switch one emoji if nothing appears.
  6. Tap the mashup. Gboard sends or inserts the sticker through the current app.

You can test popular emoji combinations when you want ideas before choosing a pair. Keep the Gboard article focused on the keyboard workflow rather than building a long list of combinations here.

How the New Browse and Favorites Experience Works

Newer Emoji Kitchen versions can make it easier to view more sticker choices instead of relying only on a few suggestions. Google’s September 2025 Android update says users can browse the Emoji Kitchen library, save favorite options, and see personalized suggestions.

Do not assume every phone will show the same Browse button. During 2026, Gboard users reported cases where the expanded browsing control disappeared while basic suggestions still worked. Some regained it after reinstalling or updating Gboard, while others saw inconsistent behavior. Those reports can help with troubleshooting, but they are not official feature guarantees.

If you have Browse or favorites, use them to keep useful reactions close. If you only see a few suggestions, the core mixing feature may still work normally.

Which Emojis Work With Gboard Emoji Kitchen?

Gboard works with supported emoji combinations rather than every possible pair. When a mix fails, changing one of the two emojis is often enough to get a result.

Good starting categories include:

  • Faces and expressions
  • Hearts and emotion symbols
  • Animals
  • Food and drink
  • Weather and nature
  • Common objects
  • Hands and gestures
  • Stars, fire, and other symbols

Some emojis also produce themed results when used with the same emoji or with a closely related symbol. Available recipes can change as Google adds new artwork, so avoid treating an old compatibility list as permanent.

Google continues to add new Emoji Kitchen sticker combinations through Android updates.

Why Is Emoji Kitchen Not Showing on Gboard?

Emoji Kitchen Gboard may disappear because Gboard is inactive, a pair is unsupported, the current app handles stickers differently, or the interface changed after an update.

Try these fixes in order:

  1. Update Gboard. Check Google Play for the current stable version.
  2. Confirm Gboard is active. Switch away from another keyboard if needed.
  3. Try another app. Open a messaging app and test the same pair again.
  4. Choose another emoji pair. Unsupported combinations will not produce a mashup.
  5. Check Gboard settings. Look for emoji, sticker, or suggestion controls.
  6. Restart Gboard or your phone. A fresh session can restore missing interface elements.
  7. Clear Gboard cache if needed. Avoid clearing storage first because that can reset app data and preferences.
  8. Reinstall only as a later step. Some users have restored missing Browse controls this way, but it is not a guaranteed fix.

If the keyboard method still fails, use the browser mixer while you troubleshoot Gboard.

Gboard Emoji Kitchen on iPhone and Other Devices

Emoji Kitchen in Gboard is mainly an Android workflow. Google does offer Gboard for iPhone, but its App Store listing focuses on features such as emoji search, GIFs, typing, and stickers rather than presenting Emoji Kitchen as the main iOS route.

For Apple devices, use a browser-based mixer instead. The dedicated Emoji Kitchen on iPhone guide covers copying, downloading, and sharing results without duplicating those steps here.

Desktop users can also use Google Search or a browser mixer. This keeps the process separate from a phone keyboard.

Tips for Better Gboard Emoji Combinations

Better results usually start with one clear idea and one supporting emoji. You do not need to test random pairs endlessly.

  • Start with a face when emotion matters most.
  • Add a heart for affection or support.
  • Pair animals with faces for playful reactions.
  • Use food, fire, stars, or weather to add context.
  • Try the same emoji twice when supported.
  • Save useful favorites if your Gboard version offers that option.

The goal is not to make the busiest sticker. Pick a pair that still makes sense at a glance.

FAQs

What is Emoji Kitchen on Gboard?

It is Google’s emoji mashup feature for Gboard on Android. It combines supported emojis into sticker-style images that you can send through compatible apps.

How do I enable Emoji Kitchen on Gboard?

Update Gboard, set it as your active keyboard, open the emoji panel, and check the emoji or sticker suggestion settings available on your version.

Why is Emoji Kitchen not showing on Gboard?

The cause may be an unsupported pair, a different active keyboard, app-specific behavior, or a Gboard interface issue. Update Gboard and test another pair and app first.

Does Emoji Kitchen work on every Android phone?

Not necessarily in the same way. Availability and layout can vary by device, Android version, Gboard version, region, and feature rollout.

Can I use Emoji Kitchen without Gboard?

Yes. Google offers Emoji Kitchen through Google Search, and browser-based mixers can also create supported mashups without changing your keyboard.

Are Emoji Kitchen mashups real emojis?

No. They are designed sticker-style images rather than new Unicode emoji characters. They may be sent as stickers or images instead of text symbols.

Why do some emoji pairs not work?

Emoji Kitchen only supports combinations for which artwork exists. Change one emoji and test another common face, heart, animal, food item, or symbol.

Can I save Emoji Kitchen favorites in Gboard?

Google announced favorite-saving as part of its newer Emoji Kitchen experience in 2025. The control may not appear identically on every device or Gboard build.

Is Emoji Kitchen available on iPhone?

You can use Emoji Kitchen on iPhone through a browser. Do not depend on the iOS version of Gboard as the main way to make mashups.
