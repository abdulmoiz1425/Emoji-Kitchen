Emoji Kitchen WhatsApp: How to Create and Send Stickers

Want to send a reaction that feels more personal than a normal emoji? Many users open WhatsApp, check the emoji panel, and cannot find the mixing feature they expected. The reason is simple: Emoji Kitchen WhatsApp stickers come from Google’s Emoji Kitchen, not from a separate WhatsApp tool.

This guide shows the quickest Android method, a browser method for iPhone and desktop users, and simple fixes when a sticker does not appear.

Quick answer: Android users can create Emoji Kitchen stickers through Gboard and send them in a WhatsApp chat. iPhone, desktop, and other users can make a mashup in a browser, then copy or download the result. The final design is a sticker-style image, not a new Unicode emoji.

What Is Emoji Kitchen on WhatsApp?

Emoji Kitchen is a Google feature that combines two supported emojis into one custom sticker. WhatsApp is the app where you share the result; it does not create the mashup itself.

Google introduced Emoji Kitchen through Gboard on Android in 2020. Google has continued adding combinations and, in 2025, added easier browsing and favourite-saving options in Gboard. In June 2026, Google announced another set of new combinations.

The result is different from a standard emoji. Unicode lists characters and approved sequences that devices render as text symbols. An Emoji Kitchen result is a designed image that usually sends as a sticker or picture.

How to Use Emoji Kitchen WhatsApp Stickers on Android

The fastest Android method is to use Gboard inside a WhatsApp conversation.

  1. Install or update Gboard from Google Play.
  2. Set Gboard as your active keyboard in the phone settings.
  3. Open WhatsApp and choose a private or group chat.
  4. Tap the message field to open Gboard.
  5. Tap the emoji icon on the keyboard.
  6. Choose an emoji that supports a mashup.
  7. Select a second compatible emoji when needed.
  8. Tap the suggested sticker to send it.

The exact layout may differ by phone model and Gboard version. Newer Gboard versions may also show a browse area or saved favourites. Google’s official Android updates describe Emoji Kitchen as a Gboard sticker-combination feature.

What if only one emoji is needed?

Some emojis produce themed variations after you select one icon. Other results appear after choosing two compatible emojis.

If no suggestion appears, switch one emoji and try a common face, heart, animal, food item, or symbol.

How to Use Emoji Kitchen for WhatsApp in a Browser

A browser mixer is the better option when Gboard is unavailable, the suggestion panel does not appear, or you use an iPhone or computer.

  1. Open the Emoji Kitchen tool in your browser.
  2. Pick the first emoji for the main mood or subject.
  3. Choose a second emoji to create the mashup.
  4. Wait for the finished sticker to appear.
  5. Select Copy for immediate sharing or Download to save it.
  6. Open WhatsApp and choose your chat.
  7. Paste the copied image or attach the saved file.

Downloading is often more dependable than pasting, especially on iPhone. The saved file can be attached from Photos, Downloads, or the device’s file picker.

How to Save and Send the Sticker

The correct sharing method depends on how WhatsApp handles the result on your device.

For Gboard, tap the suggested mashup. Gboard passes the sticker to the chat, although its appearance may differ by device or app version. Check the preview before sending, especially in a formal or work conversation.

For the browser method, try Copy first. If WhatsApp does not accept the pasted image, use Download. Open the attachment menu in WhatsApp, select the saved image, and send it like a normal picture.

A downloaded mashup does not become part of your standard emoji keyboard. Keep favourite files in a small album or folder so you can reuse them later.

Why Emoji Kitchen Is Not Showing in WhatsApp

The feature usually fails to appear because Gboard is inactive, the selected emojis do not support a mix, or the current device interface handles suggestions differently.

Try these fixes in order:

  1. Update Gboard from its official Google Play listing.
  2. Confirm that Gboard is the active keyboard.
  3. Close and reopen WhatsApp.
  4. Choose a different emoji pair.
  5. Restart the phone.
  6. Check Gboard’s emoji or sticker suggestion settings.
  7. Clear the Gboard cache as a later troubleshooting step.
  8. Use the browser method if the keyboard route still fails.

Avoid clearing Gboard storage unless necessary because it may reset keyboard preferences and save data. Google’s Android troubleshooting guidance treats clearing an app’s cache as a standard step and warns that clearing storage removes app data.

Do You Need an Emoji Kitchen App or Download?

You do not need a separate official Emoji Kitchen app for this task. Android users can use Gboard, while other users can open a browser-based mixer.

Be careful with third-party apps that use similar names. A search for “WhatsApp Emoji Kitchen download” may show unrelated apps that are not published by Google. For the official Android keyboard route, use Gboard’s Google Play listing.

Downloading usually means saving the finished PNG or image file. It does not install a new Unicode character or add the mashup to your phone’s default emoji keyboard.

FAQs

Can I use Emoji Kitchen directly in WhatsApp?

On Android, you can access its suggestions through Gboard while typing in WhatsApp. The feature belongs to Google’s keyboard, not WhatsApp itself.

Does the receiver need Gboard?

No. The receiver usually sees the result as a sticker-style image. They do not need Gboard to view an image already sent in the chat.

Can Emoji Kitchen combine three emojis?

The normal mixer combines two emojis at a time. To include a third element, you would need to edit the finished image in another image tool.

Can I use it on an iPhone?

Yes. Follow the Emoji Kitchen on iPhone guide to use Safari or Chrome, choose two emojis, and copy or download the result for WhatsApp. Do not rely on the iPhone version of Gboard as the main Emoji Kitchen method.

Why does my result send as an image?

Emoji Kitchen mashups are designed stickers, not standard Unicode characters. Sending them as images helps preserve the visual design across supported apps.

Can I save a combination for later?

Yes. Download the finished image and keep it in Photos or a dedicated folder. Some newer Gboard versions also support browsing and saving favourite combinations.

Is there an official WhatsApp Emoji Kitchen link?

WhatsApp does not provide a separate official mixer page. Use Gboard on Android or a trusted browser mixer, then share the finished result in WhatsApp.
