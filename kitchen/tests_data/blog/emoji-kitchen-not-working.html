You pick two emoji, wait for the mashup, and nothing happens. Or the sticker shows up but won't paste into your chat. Most of the time the cause is small: an app that doesn't accept stickers, an old keyboard version, or a pair that simply doesn't have a mashup yet.

This guide walks through the most common reasons Emoji Kitchen seems broken and what to try for each one, whether you use Google's version in Gboard or Search, or the mixer here on Emoji Kitchen Hub.

Quick note: Emoji Kitchen Hub (emojikitchenhub.com) is an unofficial, fan-made website. It is not run by Google and isn't affiliated with Google, Gboard, or Android. Google's own Emoji Kitchen feature is a separate product, so if something is wrong inside Gboard, Google is the one that fixes it.

First, figure out which Emoji Kitchen you're using

"Emoji Kitchen" can mean a few different things, and the fix depends on which one is giving you trouble:

  • Gboard on Android. Google's keyboard can suggest mashup stickers when you pick emoji in a supported app.
  • Google Search. On some devices and browsers, searching "emoji kitchen" on Google shows a mixer right on the results page.
  • Fan-made web mixers like Emoji Kitchen Hub. These run in your browser on phone or desktop and let you download mashups as images.

If you're on an iPhone, Gboard's Emoji Kitchen suggestions may not appear the way they do on Android. Our Emoji Kitchen on iPhone guide covers the options that work there.

Fix 1: Check that the emoji pair actually has a mashup

Not every combination exists. Emoji Kitchen mashups are designed artwork, not automatic blends, so plenty of pairs have no result at all.

Try this:

  1. Swap one of the two emoji for a different one.
  2. Try a popular base emoji like a smiley face, a heart, or an animal.
  3. Browse ready-made pairs on our emoji combos page to see combinations that already work.

If a few different pairs work and one specific pair never does, the pair is the issue, not your phone.

Fix 2: Use an app that accepts stickers

This is the most common reason Gboard mashups don't show up. Gboard only suggests Emoji Kitchen stickers in apps that can receive image stickers. In a plain text field, like a search bar, a notes app, or a username box, you'll usually only see regular emoji.

Try this: Open a messaging app that supports stickers or images, start a chat, and pick an emoji from the Gboard emoji panel there. If suggestions appear in that app but not elsewhere, everything is working as designed.

Fix 3: Update Gboard

Emoji Kitchen depends on your keyboard version. An older Gboard may not show mashups, or may be missing newer combinations.

Try this on Android:

  1. Open the Google Play Store.
  2. Search for Gboard.
  3. Tap Update if it's offered.
  4. Restart the app you're typing in and try again.

For a full setup walkthrough, see our Gboard Emoji Kitchen guide.

Fix 4: Make sure Gboard is your active keyboard

Some phones ship with a different default keyboard. Samsung phones, for example, often use Samsung Keyboard out of the box. If Gboard is installed but not selected, you won't see its mashups.

Try this: While typing, look for a keyboard switcher icon (often a small keyboard symbol near the bottom of the screen) and choose Gboard. You can also set it as the default in your phone's language and input settings. Menu names vary by phone, so look for something like "Keyboard," "On-screen keyboard," or "Input methods."

Samsung owners can find device-specific tips in our Emoji Kitchen on Samsung guide.

Fix 5: Turn on emoji and sticker suggestions in Gboard

Gboard has settings that control whether it suggests emoji and stickers. If those were switched off, mashups may never appear.

Try this: Open Gboard settings (usually by tapping the gear icon on the keyboard or finding Gboard in your phone's settings), then look in the emoji, stickers, or suggestions section for options related to emoji suggestions or stickers, and turn them on. Option names can change between Gboard versions, so look for anything mentioning emoji or sticker suggestions.

Fix 6: The mashup won't paste? Download it instead

Emoji Kitchen results are images, not regular emoji characters. That's why they sometimes refuse to paste. Many apps and text boxes only accept text, so the paste either does nothing or drops in a blank space.

Try this:

  1. Download the mashup as an image file.
  2. In your chat or social app, use the photo, gallery, or attach button.
  3. Pick the saved image and send it.

Our guide on how to save and download Emoji Kitchen stickers walks through this step by step on phone and desktop. If you're sharing in WhatsApp, the Emoji Kitchen on WhatsApp guide covers that app specifically.

Fix 7: Google Search mixer not appearing

Google's search-based mixer doesn't show up for every search, device, or browser, and Google can change or limit where it appears at any time.

Try this:

  • Search the exact phrase "emoji kitchen" on google.com.
  • Try a different browser or the Google app.
  • Make sure your browser is up to date.

If it still doesn't appear, you can use a web mixer like the one on the Emoji Kitchen Hub homepage, which works in any modern browser.

Fix 8: Emoji Kitchen Hub preview is blank or slow

If the mixer on our site isn't showing a preview, a few quick checks usually sort it out:

  • Refresh the page. A slow connection can stop the image from loading.
  • Try another pair. A blank preview can simply mean that pair has no mashup.
  • Disable aggressive content blockers for the page. Some blockers can stop images from loading.
  • Update your browser or try a different one, like Chrome, Safari, Edge, or Firefox.
  • Clear the browser cache if the page looks broken or out of date.

You can also try the same mixer from the Emoji Maker or Emoji Generator pages.

Fix 9: Restart and try again

It sounds basic, but closing the app you're typing in, or restarting your phone, can clear a stuck keyboard and bring mashup suggestions back. It's worth a try before digging into settings.

Still stuck? Use a regular emoji instead

If you need an emoji inside a sentence, a caption, or a bio, a mashup image won't work there no matter what. Use a standard emoji from our online emoji keyboard, or the emoji keyboard for PC if you're on a computer.

FAQ

Why is Emoji Kitchen not showing up on my keyboard?

The most common reasons are that you're typing in an app that doesn't accept stickers, Gboard isn't your active keyboard, Gboard needs an update, or emoji and sticker suggestions are turned off in Gboard settings.

Why does my Emoji Kitchen combination say it's not available?

Not every emoji pair has a mashup. Each combination is designed artwork, so many pairs simply don't exist. Swap one emoji and try again, or browse known working pairs on our emoji combos page.

Why won't my Emoji Kitchen sticker paste?

Mashups are images, not text characters, so many text boxes won't accept them. Download the image and send it with your app's photo or attach button instead.

Does Emoji Kitchen work on iPhone?

Gboard's Emoji Kitchen suggestions may not work on iPhone the way they do on Android. You can still make and download mashups in a browser using a web mixer. See our iPhone guide for details.

Is Emoji Kitchen Hub the official Emoji Kitchen?

No. Emoji Kitchen Hub is an unofficial, fan-made website. It isn't run by or affiliated with Google. Google's Emoji Kitchen is a separate feature in Gboard and Google Search.

Can I report a Gboard Emoji Kitchen bug to Emoji Kitchen Hub?

We can't fix problems inside Gboard, since it's a Google product. For Gboard issues, use Google's own help and feedback options. If something is wrong with the mixer on our site, you can reach us through our contact page.
