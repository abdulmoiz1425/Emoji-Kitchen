What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files that websites may store on a user's device through a web browser.

They can help websites remember information, support functionality, understand how visitors use a website, or provide and measure advertising, depending on how they are configured.

Websites may also use technologies similar to cookies for related purposes.

  • Supporting advertising
  • Measuring advertising performance
  • Improving website features and user experience

Not every category of technology described below is necessarily active on the website at all times.

You can manage or restrict cookies through your browser settings.
