How to Copy and Use These Emoji Sets

Copying a sequence should take only a few seconds.

  1. Choose a category, mood, color, or recipient.
  2. Tap the combination you want.
  3. Wait for the "Copied" confirmation.
  4. Open your message, caption, comment, or bio.
  5. Press and hold, then select Paste.
  6. Preview the result before posting or sending.

Unicode maintains official emoji characters and sequences, but platforms can draw those characters in different styles. A combination may therefore look slightly different on Apple, Google, Samsung, Microsoft, or another platform.
