How Dot Art Works

      
Each Braille character is a small block of up to eight dots arranged in two columns and four rows. The generator turns your image into a grid of light and dark points, then packs every block of two by four points into one character. A whole picture becomes a few lines of text.


      
Choosing a Mode

      
    
        
  • Dither keeps shading by mixing dots, so photos look closer to the original.
    • 
        
  • Threshold makes everything either dot or blank, which suits logos and silhouettes.
    • 
        
  • Line art traces the edges in the picture and ignores flat areas.
    • 
      


      
Getting a Better Result

      
Start with a clear picture that has a strong subject. Raise the contrast for dull photos, move the threshold if the result is too dark or too light, and use Invert when you plan to paste onto a dark background.

      
A width of 40 to 80 characters fits most chat boxes and bios. Larger sizes show more detail but may wrap in narrow places.


      
Where to Paste It

      
Dot art pastes as plain text, so it works in bios, captions and many chats. Fonts differ between apps, so check the preview before you post. For emoji pictures, use the emoji art editor, or browse ready-made emoji combos.
