Free Emoji Maker: Create Your Own Custom Emoji

Want to create your own custom emoji instead of sending a plain keyboard symbol? This free emoji maker builds a sticker-style image from two emojis you choose yourself. Pick a face, animal, heart, food, or object, then add a second emoji to change the mood or meaning. When the pair is supported, preview, copy, or download the sticker in your browser, with no sign-up.

What Is an Emoji Maker?

An emoji maker creates a custom emoji-style image. Some tools build avatars, turn a photo into a sticker, or use an AI prompt. This page is different: you choose two existing emojis and combine them into one sticker.

Emoji Kitchen Hub is an unofficial, fan-made website and is not made by Google. Use this page when you already know the idea you want. For a surprise pair, try the Emoji Generator.

How to Make Your Own Emoji

  1. Choose a Main Idea. Start with the feeling or subject you want. A face usually sets the mood; an animal, food item, or object sets the topic.
  2. Customize It With a Second Emoji. Select another emoji in the second picker. Choices with no sticker for your first emoji are greyed out.
  3. Check Your Custom Sticker. The preview shows the result when the pair is supported. If nothing appears, change one emoji and try again.
  4. Copy or Download. Use Copy to place the image on your clipboard, or Download to save a file. Some apps reject pasted images, so keep the download as a backup.

Choose Emoji Pairs That Express an Idea

Express a Feeling

Pair a warm face with a gentle symbol. Try 🥰 + 🌸 for affection or 💙 + 🌊 for a calm mood.

Make a Playful Reaction

Contrast works well for humor, such as 😱 + 👻 or 🐙 + 🍕.

Create a Themed Sticker

Stay with one theme for a planned look. 🌋 + 🔥 stays fiery, and 🍒 + 🌸 stays soft and floral. These pairs are supported; other pairs may work as well.

Can You Make an Emoji From a Photo?

No. This emoji maker combines existing emojis. It does not turn a photo into emoji artwork.

Can You Create a New Keyboard Emoji?

No. The result is a sticker-style image, not a new Unicode character. For a character you can type, use the online emoji keyboard, or see our emoji keyboard for PC guide.

Copying and Sharing Your Custom Emoji

Copy places the image on your clipboard; Download saves it as a file. For the full steps, see how to save and download Emoji Kitchen stickers. You can then add it to Instagram Stories, DMs, and posts, use it as a custom Discord emoji, or send it from an iPhone or a PC. If pasting fails, attach the downloaded file.

Troubleshooting

Unsupported Combinations

Only supported pairs produce a result. If you see "No combo found", change one emoji. Pairs in Explore Combinations are always supported.

Loading Problems

If the preview does not load, refresh the page and check your connection.

Copying Failures

Some browsers and apps block image copying. Try another browser or use Download instead.

Download Problems

If a download does not start, allow downloads from this site and try again.
