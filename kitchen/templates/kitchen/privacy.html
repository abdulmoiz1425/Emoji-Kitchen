🔒 Privacy

Privacy Policy

Last updated September 30, 2026. This page covers emojikitchenhub.com.

Emoji Kitchen Hub is an unofficial fan-made site. It is not run by Google, and it is not the Emoji Kitchen feature inside Google products.

What this site does

You can mix two emojis, copy or download a sticker, and read the blog. You do not need an account. Mashups you save in “My Combos” stay in your browser. This site does not keep a copy of them.

Ads and analytics

You do not sign in. The templates do not load a Google Analytics tag, Google Tag Manager, or an AdSense ad script. There is no gtag.js, googletagmanager script, or adsbygoogle.js on the pages.

What is in the code: the site serves an ads.txt line, google.com, pub-3200425003686406, DIRECT, f08c47fec0942fa0, and the page head includes a google-adsense-account meta tag for publisher id ca-pub-3200425003686406. That file and meta tag name an AdSense publisher. They are not a tracking script, and no ad unit is loaded. A Google Search Console verification meta tag is also present. It checks site ownership and is not visit analytics. This page does not list cookies, because the code does not define an analytics or advertising cookie list.
