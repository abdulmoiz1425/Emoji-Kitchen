How to Copy and Use a Combination

Copying a set takes only a few steps:

  1. Browse a category or search by mood, color, object, or platform.
  2. Choose the combination that fits your message.
  3. Select the Copy button.
  4. Open the app where you want to use it.
  5. Paste the set into the text field.
  6. Check the preview before posting or sending.

On a phone, press and hold inside the text field when the paste option does not appear. On a computer, use Ctrl+V on Windows or Command+V on macOS.

Why Emoji May Look Different After Pasting

Emoji are encoded as Unicode characters, but Apple, Google, Microsoft, Samsung, and other platforms draw their own artwork. Unicode Emoji v17.0 is the current released reference set; v18.0 is still in beta as of July 21, 2026.

A set may keep the same characters while its colors, shapes, or facial details look different. Joined emoji sequences can also split into separate characters on systems that do not support that sequence.

For the cleanest result, preview the set in the final app. Use common emojis when broad device support matters.

Plain Combinations vs. Emoji Kitchen Mashups

Plain emoji sequences and Emoji Kitchen mashups are not the same format.

Plain combinations can be pasted into most text fields. Emoji Kitchen creates sticker-style mixes from supported emoji pairs. Google describes its combine mode as mixing two individual emojis into a sticker.
