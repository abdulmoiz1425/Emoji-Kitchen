Make Emoji Art

Draw a picture out of emojis on a grid, then copy it as text and paste it into a bio, a caption or a chat. Pick an emoji, click or drag across the grid, and use undo, fill, crop and padding to tidy it up.

How to Make Emoji Art

  1. Choose an emoji from the palette, or type or paste any emoji into the brush box.
  2. Click or drag across the grid to draw. Use Fill to color a whole area and Erase to clear cells.
  3. Use Undo if you slip, and Crop to fit to trim empty space around the drawing.
  4. Press Copy, then paste into your app. Each grid row becomes one line of text.

Tips for Cleaner Emoji Art

Use a background emoji such as ⬜ or ⬛ for empty cells so the lines stay aligned after pasting. Keep to a few colors and fill large areas first, then add details on top.

Smaller grids paste more reliably. If an app wraps long lines, reduce the number of columns or paste into a place that does not reflow text.

What You Can Do With It

Emoji art works as a bio banner, a caption divider, a pixel-style logo or a message that stands out in a group chat. You can also import art you copied earlier, edit it, and copy it again.

For short, ready-to-paste sets, see the emoji combos collection. To turn a picture into text art, try the dot art generator. To blend two emojis into one sticker, open Emoji Kitchen.
